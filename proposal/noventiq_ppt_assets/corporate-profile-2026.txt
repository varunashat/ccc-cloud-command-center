A custom Visual Language Model (QWENVL 2.5 7B) in Bedrock analyses images to detect filled, partially empty, or empty compartments and returns bounding box coordinates. This leads to a smoother customer experience by reducing the average waiting time at the condiment station. It also results in fewer customer complaints about frequently empty compartments for specific items. / Impact; The solution accurately identifies empty or partially filled condiment compartments in real time.
